FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Does consolidation always lower my EMI?

No. It depends on interest, fees, tenure and outstanding amounts. A lower EMI may increase total interest if the tenure is longer.

Is loan approval guaranteed?

No. The lender makes the final decision after its eligibility and credit assessment.
